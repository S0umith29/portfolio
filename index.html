About

I'm Sowmith Kuppa — an M.Sc. Computer Science graduate from Old Dominion University, focused on machine learning, MLOps, and reliable AI systems.

I've built and deployed deep learning models in PyTorch and TensorFlow, designed Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines, and run data-driven experiments that improved model accuracy by 20%. My work combines AI reliability, cloud automation, and data engineering — turning complex research into production-ready systems.

Before AI, I spent years in IT consulting and systems administration, managing large-scale Windows and Linux environments, automating infrastructure with Ansible and Terraform, and driving CI/CD pipelines with Jenkins and Kubernetes. That grounding gives me a sharp intuition for real-world system reliability.

I'm passionate about responsible, reproducible AI — from data preprocessing through monitoring and model refinement — always looking to bridge research, automation, and practical impact.
